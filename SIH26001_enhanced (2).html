// Process

How the System Works

From field observations to an actionable early warning.

01 · DATA COLLECTION

Environmental Data

Rainfall, soil moisture, temperature and ground movement are collected from monitoring points.

02 · AI ANALYSIS

Risk Engine

Indicators are combined into an explainable prototype risk score.

03 · PREDICTION

Risk Forecast

Zones are classified as Low, Medium, High or Critical based on current indicators.

04 · EARLY WARNING

Action

Alerts and recommended response actions can be surfaced to authorities and communities.

// Protocol

Emergency Response

🔴 Critical

Evacuate vulnerable zones, alert authorities, restrict access and increase sensor sampling.

🟠 High

Increase monitoring frequency, notify local authorities and inspect vulnerable slopes.

🟢 Normal

Continue routine monitoring and maintain sensor connectivity and data quality.
